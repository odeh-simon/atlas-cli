# Code of Conduct

Sigil aims to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers, and be patient with questions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory or demeaning comments
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberate disruption
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and any official project channels.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participants who break this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
